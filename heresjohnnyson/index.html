Page description & dialogue

The advertisement

Chris, in a red suit and cream hat, and MJ, in a black dress and a black hat with a cream rim, stand before the Stanley Hotel beneath a red sky. The title reads “The Uncast Twins.” A caption introduces “The superior older siblings of the Stanley... and beyond.”

They look down a red hallway at two younger twins in blue dresses.

MJ: “They got a hallway.”
Chris: “We got the whole Kubrickverse.”

The older siblings stand at the foot of a grand staircase. Light spills through an open door at the top.

The Price of the Second Hat

Chris and MJ pose outside the hotel. The captions read “Scene 1: The Price of the Second Hat” and “The Stanley Hotel. A casting conversation awaits.”

A close-up of Chris carries the question “Is that the fee for both of you?” MJ replies, “That's for his hat.”

The pair maintain their composure. At a desk marked “Casting conversation,” a bespectacled man gestures toward them. In the final panel, Chris and MJ walk down the hallway, his arm around his chosen sister.

The Hallway

Under the title “Scene 2: The Hallway,” Chris and MJ face the younger twins, who stand hand in hand in matching blue dresses. The girls glare at them.

MJ: “They got a hallway.”
Chris: “We got the whole Kubrickverse.”

Chris and MJ walk arm in arm toward the reader, leaving the girls behind. In a small inset, one younger sister whispers to the other: “I hate when they do this.”

A Tour of Inferior Holdings

Chris and MJ stand before a red-carpeted staircase. The title is “A Tour of Inferior Holdings.” They survey the hotel lounge.

MJ: “Too much haunting. Not enough taste.”
Chris, holding a drink: “No discipline in the haunting department.”

The younger twins peer around a door. Chris and MJ exchange knowing smiles.

The concierge: “Ah! The senior siblings! Welcome back to the Stanley, Mr. and Ms. Uncast.” He adds, “Your inferior holdings await your continued neglect.”

Beyond the Stanley

The title reads “Beyond the Stanley.” Behind Chris and MJ, a glowing doorway waits at the top of the hotel's staircase.

MJ: “Ready?”
Chris: “Always.”

They walk side by side into the light. Beyond the doorway is an immense black-and-white geometric floor, red walls, and a black monolith beneath a red sky. The final panel returns to their faces and reads “To be continued.”
